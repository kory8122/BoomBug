Policy & terms for the BoomBug arena.

Play thoughtfully, protect your account, and keep every round fun. These simple rules explain how BoomBug works and what we expect from every player.

Ready, set, bug hunt.

BoomBug is a casual puzzle game built around observation, quick thinking, and friendly competition. By playing, you agree to the guidelines below.

🎮

How the game works

Find the target, solve each level, and use your available hearts and rewards wisely. Difficulty can increase as you progress.

  • Play levels for personal enjoyment and challenge.
  • Progress and rewards may be stored locally on your device.
  • Game features may change as BoomBug grows.
⚡

Fair play

Keep the arena welcoming. Do not abuse bugs, modify the app, automate play, or interfere with another player’s experience.

  • No cheating, exploits, reverse engineering, or unauthorized tools.
  • No harassment, hateful content, or impersonation.
  • We may restrict access when these rules are broken.
🔒

Privacy & permissions

BoomBug is designed to use only the access needed for its features. Some services may process technical information to provide ads, notifications, audio, or reliable gameplay.

Your choice matters. You can manage notifications and device permissions through your system settings.
🏆

Rewards & advertising

Optional rewarded ads may be offered for in-game benefits. Rewards are virtual, have no cash value, and may depend on a successful ad view or network availability.

  • Never pay anyone for a BoomBug reward outside the app.
  • Ad content is supplied by third parties.
  • Close an ad if it is not appropriate for you.
✉

Updates, support & acceptance

We may update these terms when the game, laws, or services change. The latest version will be published with this page. Continued play after an update means you accept the revised terms. For help, feedback, or a report, reach out through the official social channel below.
